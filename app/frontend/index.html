Catchment Water-Balance Equation: ΔS = P − ET − Q

Precipitation (P) enters the catchment; actual evapotranspiration (ET) and streamflow discharge (Q) exit. Any difference accumulates in or depletes from the effective catchment storage (S). Actual ET is rigorously bounded: 0 ≤ ET ≤ PET. Specific runoff depth is converted from volumetric streamflow via Q [mm/day] = 86.4 × Q [m³/s] / Area [km²].

Scientific Theory: Physics-Informed Machine Learning (PIML)

Hydrological rainfall-runoff modeling has traditionally relied either on conceptual, lumped bucket models (such as HBV, PDM, GR4J) or pure data-driven deep learning models (such as conventional LSTM). While deep neural networks achieve remarkable predictive accuracy, unconstrained data-driven models frequently violate fundamental physical laws: generating negative runoff, creating or destroying water mass, and suffering from catastrophic performance degradation when deployed on data-sparse basins or non-stationary regimes.

HydroPhysics AI incorporates a continuous catchment mass-conservation inductive bias:

ΔS = P − ET − Q
L_total = L_pred + λ_physics × L_water_balance

Key Physical Invariants Guaranteed

  • Streamflow Non-Negativity: Q ≥ 0 is strictly enforced via Softplus non-linear activation.
  • Actual Evapotranspiration Bound: 0 ≤ ET ≤ PET is enforced by scaling Potential Evapotranspiration by a Sigmoid fractional gate α ∈ (0, 1).
  • Moisture Storage Non-Negativity: Catchment moisture store S ≥ 0 is enforced via Softplus.
  • Dimensional Rigor: Q [mm/day] = 86.4 × Q [m³/s] / Area [km²]. Units are never conflated.

Scientific Assumptions & Limitations

  • Closed Surface Watershed: Boundary groundwater inter-basin transfers are assumed negligible.
  • Discrete Daily Steps: Discrete Euler integration approximates continuous differential dS/dt.
  • Research Prototype: Designed for scientific evaluation and Master's ML portfolio; not an operational flood alert siren.
